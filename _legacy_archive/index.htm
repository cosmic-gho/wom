Who We Are

Worldwide Oilfield Machine (WOM) is a vertically integrated, multinational oilfield equipment manufacturer  specializing in custom solutions for the drilling, testing, production and intervention segments of the oil and gas industry.

Established in 1980 with headquarters based in Houston, Texas, our WOM group of companies employ 3,000+ employees in strategically located facilities around the world. As both a minority- and female-owned enterprise, our diverse workforce supports manufacturing plants, engineering centers, sales offices, and assembly and testing locations across 10 countries.

 

Our deep roots in material science and forging enable a holistic approach to design and engineering—the equipment is ours from concept and design to finished product. 

 

We specialize in continual innovation in wellhead, BOP and valve technology for the upstream and midstream markets. We work closely with our customers to develop meaningful solutions to support their most challenging scenarios.

Innovative subsea capabilities

We’re about driving new technological developments, especially in our subsea initiatives. WOM proactively responds to the industry’s evolving subsea needs with products such as our patented DRS-II and Compact Cutting Device. Our global network of facilities and in-house capabilities allow us to deliver the subsea production and intervention equipment our customers need, when they need them.

Industry-leading BOP technology

Our BOP products are designed to be compatible with most other major brands for seamless integration into existing equipment. This includes interchangeable aftermarket parts, such as our full line of OEM elastomer products. WOM’s fully integrated manufacturing line allows for complete control to ensure compliance to all API regulations and customer-specific requirements.

Next-level gate valve technology

Leveraging our existing valve technology with decades of field-proven reliability, the Magnum SP gate valve was engineered with industry and environmental needs in mind. It features advanced sealing technology in an optimized, modular design. Our ground-breaking gate valve delivers reliable performance with unmatched advantages, such as reduced footprint, less weight, and grease requirements.
